# Teach a subject

Use this when the user wants to learn something, be taught a subject, or practise until they can explain why a step follows.

1. Locate the starting point: ask what the learner already knows and what they want to be able to do.
2. Weave one link at a time: a short explanation, one worked example, then one check the learner answers before you continue.
3. Build up: after each link, ask for the learner's own restatement in their words. Mark it accurate, needs repair, or a knowledge gap — and repair before adding the next link.
4. Keep a notebook for the thread: terms, open questions, and diagrams the learner asked for.
5. Verify any fact you are unsure about with `web_search` or `web_fetch` before teaching it. Write in the learner's language.
